Music that Takes your Breath Away

My Approach

I have successfully composed music from the ground-up to a multitude of projects. I utilize my classical training and apply it to whatever genre makes the subject material pop. From chilling solo piano pieces to vibrant electronic dance tunes, I'm not afraid to mix things up and delight listeners in unexpected ways.

I create most of my music using synthesized instruments, either ones purchased from world-class sample artists or synthesizers that I have purchased myself. If the need arises, I am also well-versed in recording performances to add that graceful human touch. I believe in iterative processes, and try to work as closely as I can with my clients to ensure they get the sound they want and deserve.

While my style often changes to adapt to the nature of the project, I believe in strong melodies that stick with listeners long after they're done listening. I love writing music, and I strive to bring smiles to my listeners.
