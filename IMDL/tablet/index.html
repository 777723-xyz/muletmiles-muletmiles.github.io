About the bot

[UNTITLED ROBOT] will be an autonomous robotics project completed by me, Miles Mulet, for the Intelligent Machine Design Laboratory at the University of Florida. Why are there no details? Because I haven't decided what to build yet! I have a couple of ideas so far, and have made it my goal to finalize the idea and preliminary design by

Friday, January 15, 2016.

Current Ideas

Explorer Bot

Similar to the Security Bot, except the Explorer Bot looks for unknown objects in an unknown space (i.e., looking for new things I guess). Probably would be hard...

Sound-Making Bot

I have lots of experience in sound/audio engineering. Maybe I could work this into a robot somehow, maybe communicating via music?

Ball Catching Robots

An idea that I've had is to create 2 identical (or nearly identical) robots that played "catch" with each other and worked to throw an object from one to the other in any location.

Blanket Bot

So, I won't do this, but I kinda had a funny idea of a blanket-bot that would monitor your feet at night and make sure they don't get too cold by keeping the blanket over them.

Security Bot

A small robot that checks sensitive locations in a known environment for change. If it detects a change, it reports this to a server.

Mapping Robot

A robot that would communicate directly with a computer to traverse and map out a space. I'm kinda leaning on no for this one...
