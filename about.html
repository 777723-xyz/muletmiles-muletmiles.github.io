A Quick Bio:

Hello! My name's Miles Mulet and I love to bring creative projects to life. I have extensive experience composing music for movies and videogames, as well programming a plethora of applications for a variety of devices. Some day I'd like to see myself complete projects in computer simulations, embedded systems, and (to a lesser extent) big data analysis that have a lasting positive impact on the world. For specific info, please see my resume.

I thrive working in teams with big ambitions, and I live to accept challenging projects. If you have an endeavour that you'd like me to help with, don't hesitate to reach out to me!
