Inventive Games that Take you to New Worlds

My Approach

I think of video games as an art form; there's so many fantastic experiences that consumers can have that are only possible in an interactive experience. That being said, I recognize that the number one focus for a video games should always be fun, so I place effort into creating games that make players and are also a blast to play.

The technology that I use varies depending on the target platform, but I typically use the Unity3d engine to deliver finished products. I usually write C# scripts as the backbone of the logic required by games, and I'm not afraid to dig into some lower-level c++ code to deliver as polished an experience as possible.
